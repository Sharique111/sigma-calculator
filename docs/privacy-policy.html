Sigma Calculator Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026 · Developer: Stars&Rockets

This policy explains how the Sigma Calculator app for Android ("Sigma", "the app") handles information. In short: Sigma does not collect, transmit or share any personal information.

Information the app collects

None. Sigma does not ask for an account, does not request any device permissions, and does not connect to the internet. We do not receive any information about you or how you use the app.

Information stored on your device

To be useful, Sigma saves a few things locally on your phone only:

  • Your calculation history (up to the 50 most recent calculations)
  • Your last answer, used by the Ans key
  • Your settings: degrees or radians, and light or dark appearance

This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. You can delete your history at any time with the Clear button in the History panel. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, removes all of it.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

Sigma contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting tools and no other third-party services that collect data. We do not sell, rent or share any information.

Google Play

Sigma is distributed through Google Play. When you download or update the app, Google may collect information as described in Google's Privacy Policy. That processing is controlled by Google, not by us.

Children

Sigma does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
